---
name: account-brief
description: Prepare a customer account brief or meeting preparation by connecting account history, stakeholders, usage, support, and open actions.
---

Read [the evidence and connection contract](../../references/evidence-and-connections.md) before retrieving customer information.

Resolve the account, audience, meeting purpose, and time window. Start from the latest account plan and CRM record; inspect linked discussions and documents for material changes. Retrieve usage, support, and feedback only where they answer the meeting's questions.

Produce a concise brief:

- Current goals, account team, customer sponsor/champions, and renewal timing when verified.
- What changed: adoption, business outcomes, stakeholder engagement, support blockers, and sentiment. Show evidence dates and conflicting signals.
- Open commitments with owner, due date, status, and original source. Omission from a newer summary does not establish completion.
- Three useful discussion questions and the next actions they could unlock.

For NPS or feedback themes, state the response count, comment count, period, and sample limitations; distinguish respondent perception from verified product behavior. Finish with missing inputs that could change the meeting plan. Keep inferred stakeholder roles clearly labeled.
