---
name: account-handoff
description: Prepare a source-linked customer account transition or CSM handoff with relationship context, commitments, risks, and next steps.
---

Read [the evidence and connection contract](../../references/evidence-and-connections.md). Identify the outgoing and incoming scope, transition date, and current account plan.

Review the CRM account, recent account conversations, current success plan, and unresolved support work. Prefer the organization's current handoff template when accessible.

Draft a handoff covering:

- Account identity and scope, account team, sponsor, champions, and stakeholder gaps.
- Customer objectives, deployment and adoption state, verified value, and relevant contract dates.
- Relationship context and communication preferences supported by recent evidence.
- Risks and blockers with severity evidence and the current mitigation owner.
- Open promises and next meetings: action, owner, due date, status, and source.
- A proposed first-month agenda for the incoming owner, separating agreed actions from suggestions.

Check each commitment against the original thread or meeting record. Highlight stale or contradictory information for the outgoing owner to resolve. Create or update a Slack Canvas or other shared document only when requested and supported; otherwise return a ready-to-paste draft. Preserve the established artifact link on later updates.
