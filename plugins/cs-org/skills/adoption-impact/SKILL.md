---
name: adoption-impact
description: Analyze customer adoption or usage changes around training, onsites, workshops, campaigns, or product rollout events.
---

Read [the evidence and connection contract](../../references/evidence-and-connections.md). Confirm the requested event source, account/site scope, cohort, and metric definitions before querying.

Inventory every event within the requested scope. Record inclusion, exclusion, duplication, cancellation, ambiguous identity, and incomplete baseline or follow-up windows. Keep this accounting even when the final output shows only a subset.

Choose comparable pre/post periods with complete coverage and a stated timezone. Exclude the event period when that avoids mixing exposure with follow-up. Flag nearby interventions, seasonality, reporting changes, and partial periods. Query narrow aggregate windows first.

Measure breadth and depth where definitions support them: active users, sustained usage, meaningful feature activity, or business outcomes. Check whether user classifications are dated snapshots or today's labels applied retrospectively. The latter cannot establish historical upgrades. Show absolute change and percentage change; percentage change from a zero baseline is undefined.

Use clear before/after charts with consistent units and scales. Include flat and negative results. Separate account-wide effects from attendee-level effects; use attendee cohorts only with verified identity mapping and permitted access.

Deliver the observed changes, coverage/exclusion ledger, definitions, and reproducible calculation details. Describe association unless the design supports causal inference. End with the practical follow-up and the evidence needed to test it.
