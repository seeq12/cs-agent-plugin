---
name: customer-retrospective
description: Create a concise customer retrospective, QBR or EBR narrative, or value slide from verified outcomes, adoption, support, and next steps.
---

Read [the evidence and connection contract](../../references/evidence-and-connections.md). Confirm the audience, reporting period, desired artifact, and approved template. Read the actual reference slide or deck before matching its layout.

Build a small evidence table before writing: claim, metric definition, period, source, refresh date, and confidence. Verify arithmetic and align comparison periods. Include material signals relevant to the story, such as meaningful usage depth, feature adoption, support, and enablement; explain unavailable measures instead of filling them with zero.

Lead with the customer's objective and the verified outcome. Use a few metrics that support that story, explain what changed, and close with agreed next actions or clearly marked proposals. Keep activity measures separate from business value. Estimated savings need assumptions and an identified source; only validated savings may be presented as realized value.

For slides, use the organization's approved template and brand resources when supplied. Keep text minimal and place definitions, source links, and reporting periods in notes. Use editable charts/shapes when the available presentation tool supports them. Render and inspect the result before delivery and report any native-app checks that remain undone.

If presentation tooling is unavailable, return slide-ready copy and chart specifications, identifying that no deck was generated. Keep internal-only account commentary out of customer-facing output.
