---
name: account-recovery
description: Investigate an at-risk or red customer account and recommend an evidence-backed recovery plan, using comparable historical accounts when available.
---

Read [the evidence and connection contract](../../references/evidence-and-connections.md). Confirm the account, risk assessment date, health definition, and specific deterioration before recommending intervention.

Build a dated timeline connecting usage, business outcomes, stakeholder changes, support issues, and commercial events. Verify who owns the account and distinguish a health score from the underlying signals. Classify potential drivers as evidenced or hypothetical; a low score alone does not establish why an account is at risk.

If useful history is available, select comparable accounts by stage, size, deployment, and risk pattern. Verify the actions actually taken, when they happened, and the subsequent outcomes. Include unsuccessful or inconclusive examples. A historical resemblance supports a candidate intervention, not a promised recovery.

Return:

- The strongest risk signals, with source, date, and remaining uncertainty.
- A short recovery plan: action, proposed owner, timing, measurable checkpoint, and why it fits this account.
- Historical precedents and limits, or an explicit statement that none were verified.
- Escalation triggers and the next review date as proposals unless already agreed.

Use the organization's current escalation policy when available. Draft escalation communication only if requested; research does not authorize contacting the customer or changing account health.
