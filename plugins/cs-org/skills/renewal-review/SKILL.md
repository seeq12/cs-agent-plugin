---
name: renewal-review
description: Prepare a customer renewal readiness review connecting contract timing, verified entitlements, usage, realized value, risks, and mutual next steps.
---

Read [the evidence and connection contract](../../references/evidence-and-connections.md). Confirm the account, contract period, renewal opportunity, and current approved renewal playbook.

Reconcile the CRM renewal record with contract evidence. Verify start/end dates, purchased units, usage definitions, procurement lead time, stakeholder approval path, and account-team ownership. Flag missing or conflicting records instead of inferring entitlements from observed feature use.

Compare licensed units and qualifying usage only when they share scope, unit, and time window. Distinguish standard/light or equivalent classifications using current business definitions. If asked for a contract-start usage reset, label it a scenario and state its start date, qualification rules, and limits; it is not a change to contractual entitlement or official reported usage.

Summarize realized outcomes, adoption gaps, support/commercial risks, and evidence supporting potential expansion. Keep expansion possibilities separate from committed opportunities or forecasts.

Return a short readiness checklist and mutual action table with owner, due date, dependency, and source. Identify the next customer discussion and unresolved commercial questions. Pricing, discounting, contract changes, and CRM writes require the corresponding user request and authority.
