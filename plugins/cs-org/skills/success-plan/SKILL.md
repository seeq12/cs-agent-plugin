---
name: success-plan
description: Create or update a living customer success plan that links business outcomes, adoption milestones, stakeholders, and mutual actions.
---

Read [the evidence and connection contract](../../references/evidence-and-connections.md). Locate the current plan and approved organizational template before drafting.

Identify the customer's stated goals, rollout scope, available data, champion capacity, and current adoption stage. Separate the customer success plan from any purchased services package; verify entitlements before describing included services.

Build a compact plan with:

| Outcome | Baseline and source | Target and date | Customer owner | Team owner | Next milestone | Evidence of completion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include the use cases and enablement needed to reach each outcome, technical dependencies, stakeholder communication cadence, and a mutual action list. Mark unagreed targets, dates, and ownership as proposed. Choose milestones from the customer's timeline and current approved playbook rather than inventing a universal renewal schedule.

For an update, reconcile prior actions against explicit completion evidence, retain unresolved items, and explain material changes. Preserve the plan's existing location and edits. Deliver the requested draft or authorized update, its source links, and the smallest set of decisions needed from the account team.
