# Proposed first contents

These are design proposals. Only `cs-setup` exists in this version.

1. **Account brief and evidence retrieval.** Combine authorized account history, discussions, opportunities, and current enablement material into a short cited brief. Start here: useful across roles with few write dependencies. Include source dates and distinguish evidence from inference.
2. **Trusted usage and health analysis.** Reuse approved metric definitions, account identifiers, reporting windows, and freshness checks. Show how numbers were calculated and expose conflicting definitions. Verify synthetic examples before adding live queries.
3. **Living success plans.** Find the existing canonical plan, propose a dated update, preserve human edits, and connect goals to owners, next actions, and outcomes. Start with reviewable changes; choose the plan destination before implementing writes.
4. **Customer-ready reviews and presentations.** Turn cited research and validated metrics into concise meeting briefs, QBRs, and editable slides using authorized templates. Keep internal-only evidence separate from customer-facing claims. Test rendering and trace every numeric claim to its source.
5. **Risk and expansion research.** Compare trends with qualitative evidence and historical precedents, then suggest actions with confidence limits. Begin on demand; scheduled alerts need separately configured audiences and authorization.

Shared across these workflows: brief setup, source citations, freshness checks, explicit missing-data handling, and a GitHub feedback path. Keep personal memories and customer data outside the public package.
